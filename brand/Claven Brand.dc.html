The two cut edges are strictly parallel. The left piece keeps the square’s full left edge; the right piece keeps the full right edge — so the silhouette still reads as one square. Never round the corners of the pieces and never change the cleave angle.

Voice

Terse
“Opened 2.1 GB in 0.8s.” Not “We’re excited to help you open large repos!”
Mechanical, not magical
Claven says what it did and how long it took. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no “oops”.
Lowercase in-product
Commands and status read lowercase (“split editor right”); marketing headlines use sentence case.
